2009-05-31

Koff, Satu & Jaga... Jimbotours drinking kicks off!

24 photographs

Following on from our random FA Cup final experience with the Finnish division of the Chelsea Headhunter`s we decided enough was enough in Helsinki and made our way to Talin - Estonia...  A cheeky local beer (Satu) on the ferry set us up for the arrival of G-Budd!

Got checked in to our Hostel which is complete with its own music lounge!  Shauny G`s heaven... though it almost went sour very quickly when I swiftly managed to break a string on one of their guitars.  Bit of the Brit charm and we were back in the good books.

Headed into town for a wander and grabbed a couple of beers in the most moody looking Irish pub in history - bar staff just didn`t want to know - and found out that another cheeky footie bet had come in courtesy of G-Budd`s vast football knowledge... more dough for the whip!

Enter the arrival of the Austrian Prince into the wierd and wacky world of Jimbotours... a quick shower, a stop off for a Rat Burger (Talin has a lot to learn about fastfood - 2/10) and we were in for a large one!

Headed straight for the Beer House for a Stein of the local brew which was rudely interupted by a member of the pissed Russian mafia equipped with his 500euro sunglasses who fancied a taste of Johnny`s beer which prompted a swift departure...

We headed North up the street to get away from the beer thieving Russain and stumbled on to a live jazz band who were well up for it... G-Budd & Johnny felt right at home cos the locals all danced just like them - crazy kids...

Bumped into the lads who worked at our hostel along with Gary Neville and Gok Wan lookalikes and headed on for a late night drink in a proper local bar...  G-Budd recruited a Korean into the East London team, Shauny G got told by tour leader of the year 2009 that his iPhone was gay and Gary Neville looked well moody.

Stopped off for a Jacket Potato and ropey hotdog and made for bed... apart from Shuny G who had a dude in his bed already - game over!

Feeling remarkably fresh this morning and getting ready for our next stop via plane... where we end up only Jimbo knows!!

Roll on stop number 3!

Shaun y G

2009-10-21

G Budd - Jimbo Tours (Solo Style - Part 1: Kaunas)

0 photographs

Good Afternoon,

Yes its true, after having so much fun on the hugely successful Jimbo Tours, I have now decided to fly solo and branch out myself.

Just spending 5 nights in Lithuania, catch up on old times (well 4 months ago), enjoy the history and carry on sampling the excellent food and cheap bier.

Arrived in Kaunas on Weds at about 12. Was on the number 29 bus from the airport, went right  through the town centre and ended up in the arse end of nowhere (it was a bit reminiscent of our last visit here - bloody scary and no-one seemed to speak Angleterre!).

Managed to find the city via foot (a lot of walking and with no pretzels!!) Then spent over 3 hours looking for a hostel (there are only 2 in the city), couldn't find either, gave up and ended up booking into a religious guesthouse - it was about 30 quid a night :(

But my whole body was knackered and i was bored of trying to find a hostel. It is times like this that i then realise how much planning goes into a proper Jimbo Tour and that i am one of life's followers, not leaders.

Kaunas itself was pretty boring and non descript. Very few bars, about 3 casinos (i didn't go in Shaun), English at a raw premium yet the birds are different gravy - and all seem to wear knee high boots, have glasses and long hair. i dunno wot they stick in the water round there but Ilford should purchase some of it! By the way i saw just 2 women, of ethnic minority, out there and both of them were fit!

Weds nite went like this - ended up in a restaurant, ate roast pork with flank (?) - bloody tasty, got talking to some fellas at the bar (one of whom was a delivery driver in Wolverhampton) and they got me into a students nite club. They kept buying me drinks as well (and i don't think they were drugged), so it was a right result.

Thurs went to plan. Spent most of the day walking around, visited 2 musems and a presidential palace, had loads of kwaffee, got back to the guesthouse about 6 ish. Had a rest, went out, ate more food, with the odd bier and then came back early. I was conscious of the next leg of the trip, so thought i'd take it easy.

So thats it so far, arrived in Vilnius at 1pm. It took about an hour and 40 mins from Kaunas (its 100km) on the coach, some of the roads definitely need some Irish labour. Checked into a hostel about half an hour ago, seems a bit weird but its got a bar so it can't be all bad.

Anyway that's all for now, updates to follow...

2009-10-23

G Budd - Jimbo Tours (Solo Style - Part 2: Vilnius)

0 photographs

So this is the second city.

Remember it well from the last time we were here in June. Much more livelier than Kaunas. Definitely more going on.

So Friday afternoon, checked into A Hostel, near the bus station. 34 Litas a night, in a dorm of 8. Not bad value, but the overall impression wasn't a good one. All the staff seemed to be over 45, the toilet/shower room always stank and the only regular in a strange looking common room, was an old bloke who used to watch films in Lithuanian - there didn't appear to be much life there.

It's a bit weird travelling around on your own, especially if u don't know the lingo. Some old fella the other day spent 5 mins talking to me in stern Russian (I had no idea what he was going on about) and all I did was nod and smile at him. For all I know, he was probably threatening to murder me! Got myself familiar with the surroundings again (and I'm afraid Shaun, that Tony Soprano has gone), I know this city better than I know Ilford now. Spent  most of the nite in Bar Bobo, got chatting to a couple of locals - an old guy Edmund, who spoke hardly any English and kept calling me a 'Strong Englishman' (i soon dispelled that theory) and a younger guy who seemed to be the Lithuanian A. Kiely (he understood me enough for a conversation and was a popular man around the ladies). Left about 1am, forgetting that to get to the hostel, was like walking through Ilford late at night.

Next day (Sat), I put Jimbo Walking Tours to shame. I must have walked for at least 6 solid hours, went in numerous churches, up and down the castle (no flip flops this time Shaun) and visited the worst Wax Works museum in the whole of Eastern Europe. Robbie Coltrane was thin, Cliff Richard looked like Elton John and John Travolta was an asian! At least Hitler looked like a Nazi! Came back, absolutely shattered, had a blow, got changed and went out for the night. It was interesting, that I met an English stag party (all in their 40's) that were from Bolton, they had travelled from Manchester Airport with Lufthansa (via Frankfurt and Tallinn), their journey took the best part of 15 hrs. Thankfully London flies direct!

But it was at this point I'd realised I'd made 2 errors, which I will remember for future tours. 1) I should have moved hostel, cos it was crap basically and there was no life or interaction there and 2) I forgot the clocks went back, meaning I had an extra hour. By the time I'd got to Prospekto Bar/Club I was so cream crackered, I could only muster it for a little bit, then went back through the dark alleys. I realise now i should have planned the whole day better, still its all experience.

And now the last day...
