About Jimbotours

Jimbotours is a personal travel diary archive spanning 2007 to 2025. This new site preserves the diary entries and photographs originally published on GetJealous.

The rescued archive contains 113 diary entries and 1421 photographs, organised here by trip and date.

This version is self-contained: the photographs are stored inside the website folder, so the archive can be backed up or moved without relying on the original GetJealous website.
