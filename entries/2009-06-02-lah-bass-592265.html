WHAT A LOADA BALTICS  ·  31 photographs

Thats Local for hello.

We justy about to leave Vilnius for Riga so thought i'd drop a quick line.

We arrived here at 11oclock at night after a lovely flight from Thalinn. Got the wrong bus - missed our stop and ended up in a war torn council estate at midnight looking like americans. Luckly we got a cab and didn't die.

Hostel been wicked - met a canadian guy called Sandy who's a top dude and works in Canary Wharf and a Dutch guy who is G Budd's favourite friend ever.

Highlights of vilnius include:

- Jimbotour of Vilnius - some uninterested people here.

- Lots of rain

- didnt go to bed until sunrise every morning - feeling sh4t now!!

- G Budd turned 33 (today) Happy Birthday

- Had wicked lithuanian food - lots of meat.

- went to a casino - Jimbo won big - shaun left it behind.

- me and g budd went shooting in the forest 45mins outta town- AK47's, Uzie's, Pistols etc. Wicked.

- General drinking - cant remeber much more!!

G budd has promised to do a birthday blog when we arrive in Riga. TBC

Jimbo
