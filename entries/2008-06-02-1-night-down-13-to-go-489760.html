2008-06-02

1 Night Down... 13 to go!

BERLIN OR BUST  ·  50 photographs

The first BIG drink of the tour is complete!  Its was always going to be a messy start when our drinks of choice for the evening were Staropromen and Jagameister!

Started off with a few beers near the hostel and slowly worked our way through a mixture of local Slovak haunts, Mexican bars, The Dubliner (we were always gonna have one in there!) and finshed up on boat club.

Highlights of the evening included Stevie B going all Ricky Hatton at Johnny, interesting graffiti on bar walls (Dont tell Sally!), bouncers asking Shauny to limbo under his tree trunk legs and Stevie B doing his best to wake up the Russian dude when we got in whilst hiding his stuff under Jimbos jeans - Arthur wasnt impressed in the morning!

Weve been all over Bratislava today - Cathedrals, the bridge Tower 95metres high and have had more coffees than meals since we left Ilford 48hrs ago!  Loose backdoors all round!

Checking into a new hostel for the tonight (Stevie was scared that Arthur might come after him!) and getting ready for another session with the locals!

Plenty for stories and pictures to come...

Shauny G.
