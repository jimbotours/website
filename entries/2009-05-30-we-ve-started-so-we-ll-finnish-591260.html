WE'VE STARTED SO WE'LL FINNISH

BERLIN OR BUST  ·  29 photographs

good title heh??

So we arrived in Helsinki. The weather is good. We`re staying in a hostel which is actually in the Olympic stadium where the 1952 games were held. We haven`t done any 100 metre naked runs yet but the girl at reception who met me on the way to the shower reckons I could do the pole vault all by myself!!

Anyway...we haven` t seen much of the city as when we arrived ahd to go straight to watch the fottball in a Chelsea pub, full of chelsea supporters and chelsea scarfes and chelsea flags..... so yeah it was full of pikies - Shaun loved it! Met a few weird chelsea fans in there and we had to escape before they showed us any more tattoo`s.

Then we went on a big of a pub crawl, played pool, drunk more, and went home to bed. Was a good night.

Its amazing over here as the sun didn`t go down till 11pm last night so was light till about mid-night then the sun comes up again at 3am. Mad

Will stick some photos up asap...speak soon.

Jimbo - East
