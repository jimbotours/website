Budapest Action So Far

BERLIN OR BUST  ·  4 photographs

The Road to Berlin continues down the Danube to Budapest! 

On our first day round Budapest Shauny & Jonny headed to the Castle and walked practically the entire length of the city (feeling extra sweaty right now!) whilst Jimbo & Stevie got close and personal with the Danube on a cruise!

We`re all gearing up for a BIG drink in Budapest after our half time break after arriving last night.  We`ve scoped out the drinking situation out and it looks like we`re heading to a jazz club tonight and an open air party tomorrow... but given our track record we could end up anywhere!

Lots of reports and new pics to come...
