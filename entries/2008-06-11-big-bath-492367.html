BIG BATH

BERLIN OR BUST  ·  7 photographs

Bang - long day.

Last night we went for a couple in a local bar. I couldnt open the door to the toilet and to the amazement of a couple of locals at the bar a little old lady had to open it for me. When I came out the local had a shot of UNICUM ready for me - he said it would make me strong. It tasted rough as f*** - so we had another!!

Then went to a pub called Old mans bar - they put the prices up by 100% coz we were tourists - fuckers - so we left.

Today me and steve went on a 4 and a half hour bile tour round Budapest. Very good but very sweat in 100f heat so we were well ready for what we all did next - a visit to a Budapest Baths and Spa. This was wicked - the feeling when you get into a 38c bath was amazing!!

Went to a steam room which was bloody hot - steve left first as it blinded him followed by me john and Shaun managed a good bit - then we all plunged into a freezing pool - wow. Before we left we had to have a Bidit which was lts saz a refreshing experience!! This was followed by a massively Posh packing meal and back to the hostel for a blow.

The road to Berlin comes at 12pm tommorrow with a flight and get on it!!
