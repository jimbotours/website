The Final Furlong...

BERLIN OR BUST  ·  0 photographs

We`ve touched down in Berlin.

Quiet night last night means we`re ready to tear Berlin apart... looks like we`ve found Germanys answer to Vienna`s fanzone so we`re all over that tonight for Germany v Croatia then heading to a pub crawl tomorrow.

I predict plenty of Jaga`s (Stevies already one down for losing Shaunys last bag off the carosel game at the airport - GREAT GAME!), big dabs, Stevie getting up close and personal with our new room mates last tour style (the Koreans weren`t happy... who knows how this lot will react!) and a sprint to finish like the Grand National has never seen before.

Watch out Berlin the London/Lurgan connection are following Shaunys new motto "Win or lose we`re on the booze!"

Reports to come...

Shauny G
