2009-06-06

7 Days - 4 Cities

WHAT A LOADA BALTICS  ·  37 photographs

Just done it - from what i can remeber.

We kicked off our last day in Riga with a late start after another heaving night ending at 7 in the morning after nailing many more 60p beers and shot's. A long walking tour through the street ended with a nice beer in the park followed by a wicked lativan meal.

We headed into town for one last night to a cool bar where you pour your own beer - just pay 50p for the plastic glass. G budd got on the dance floor and didn't come off it till 4 hours later after dancing half of Riga into submission!!

Another 4am finish was rounded off with another Hessburger and 4 hours sleep before our taxi picked us up to go to the airport.

8am soon came. Me and G Budd would still be in bed now if it wasn't for John waking us up! Tough plane journey which was packed full off stags do's including a load of welsh one's who were producing the most discusting smells ever - tough journey!!

Got home and had a big blow. Was a great break. with all the travelling, walkign tours and drinking it merges into hazy memories so i'm glad we've got evidence to remember it by.

Spoke to Jimbotour veteran Stevie B earlier (remember him??) who's already planning JimboTours 2010 - keep tuned for details.
