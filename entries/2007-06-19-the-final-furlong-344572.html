2007-06-19

The Final Furlong!

EAST MEETS EAST  ·  5 photographs
Munich & Prague down only Krakow to go! It was a loooong train ride in from Prague - 8 hours in total! I was cooked already cos Stevie B was snoring like a beast the night before and I got no sleep! By the time we made it in to Krakow beer was needed big time! We headed out into town to see if we could find a beer and Jimbo a bride (the latter was slightly trickier!) Ended up touring most of Krakows Irish pubs - Stevie B was in his element talking all sorts of nonsense to the locals! One guy came over to ask him if he knew the football score and Stevie B ended up telling him how to get your phone to work using chicken wire, needless to say the conversation ended pretty sharply! The beer out here is STRONG - this Zywiec stuff is loony juice! After 2 pints Johnny Boy had a wobble on! We moved on to a local pub and were greeted by a seriously wierd looking bar man! A couple more Zywiec`s and we decided to get a game of coins in the barman loaded up a rotten looking shot and as usual Shauny G lost! The barman wasn`t happy though - he really wanted John to have the shot! So far Jimbo & Steve have managed to duck out of the shots so we played again and it ended up Jimbo v Stevie heads up! Stevie B lost and I swear it looked like his head was gonna expload when he did the shot! It got too wierd in the end once the barman started putting porn on the TV we were out of there quick time! Back at the hostel Stevie B was sleeping on the floor and trying to get into every other bunk bed than his (the Koreans didn`t look happy in the morning!) I get the feeling we won`t be chilling out in the last few days of this tour - tonight BOOZE CRUISE! BANG! Shauny G!
