Berlin Day 2

BERLIN OR BUST  ·  0 photographs

Big walk yesterday to start our Berlin trip, it involved looking at lots of pictures of buildings that are not there anymore

Amazing city, the tour guide even knew Lurgan (lost a point for that)

He got 7 out of 10, mostly because he sent us to a really cool area of Berlin for our evening that meant we coulld avoid all of the british stag dos - they get everywhere!

Watched the Dutch French game in a Beer Garden which was full of their natives, great atmosphere and Stevie was happy because he got a pretzal!

Moved on to the bars round the corner after the game and stumbled across a couple of jems, very Shoreditch, listened to some funky jazz and met some locals.  Shortly after, we went to what looked someone`s front room where we were made very welcome and celebrated the hottest girl in theres birthday

Stevie had women all round him, he didn`t know which way to look - unfortunately, the cab would only take the 4 of us home

In bed at 4.30 - big sleep and walked the city again today

James and Stevie have met a strange Brazilia girl who is apparently joining us tonight - more stories and photos to follow

Until then
