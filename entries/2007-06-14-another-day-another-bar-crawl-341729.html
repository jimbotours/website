2007-06-14

Another day another Bar Crawl!

EAST MEETS EAST  ·  14 photographs
Yesterday was a loooong day! Eventually the 3 amigo`s got out of bed and believe me it wasn`t a pretty sight! After much debating it was decided that the only thing left to do was get back on it and head for bar crawl number 2! The guy leading us about was this loud Aussie and it was pretty clear his intention was to get us ruined! A bit of history about German beer and we were set! This guy filled us with wheat beer & Jaegermeister - for those of you that don`t know what Jaegermeister is think black Sambuca on speed!!!! It was pissing it down but we soldiered through in true poshpacking style! We had a beer in the BIGGEST beer garden in the world - it seats 9,000 - more like a beer field!!! By this time "Callum" was in full throttle and the ladies were flocking from every corner of the garden for their chance to get their 5 minutes of fame with the living legend! Ended up the same way it always does plenty more beer, Johnny getting in with the Yankees, Shauny G & Jimbo sampling fine Turkish cuisine, more beer, Stevie (sorry Callum) cooked in the top bunk! I think its safe to say that we made our mark in Munich (not sure if they`d have us back!) Watch out Prague the lads are on their way!!! Get the beer cold and let the Yankees know John`s on route! Next stop Prague - BANG! Shauny G. p.s Stevie recommends a pretzel for a hangover cure!
