G Budd, from Austrian Prince to Lithuanian Count

WHAT A LOADA BALTICS  ·  0 photographs

Hi everyone,

Wow, i dunno where to start.

First of all thanx to all my fans for wishin my a good birthday.

its been absolute class, definitely one of the best birthdays ever - well its in the top 33!

In the last few days - it feels like weeks - but thats wot u get with Jimbo tours. Pure adventure and excitement all the way

I've been firing AK 47's and Magnums (not the ice cream), avoided bein killed by the Russian Mafia about 3 times, met a gr8 Dutch guy, Arno, who knows everything about non league footy, don't worry big fella u'll see Orient nxt season and had so much different meat from roasted chicken livers to bull stomach soup to zeppelins filled with meat (the size of them aint human!

anyway i'm seriously thinkin of movin out here, quote me on that Ross and i wish i was here longer!

its been groovy, god bless the Baltics and god bless Jimbo tours!
