X Factor Tour Touches Down In Croatia

THE ROAD TO ROME  ·  77 photographs

After our whistle stop visit to Trieste (Harrys Bar was a saver) we
arrived in Slovenia late. A quick cleanup and we were out to see
what Loseyabannana had to offer. Between moody bouncers and salsa
dancing it didn't seem like much was happening so we banged in a few
shots and got back to the hostel. Jonny is clearing missing married
life on tour and popped over to my bunk for a midnight cuddle... How
he got there nobody knows... CCTV holds the answers....

We woke up to a crackhead of a hostel worker who looked like she had
an even heavier night than us so we packed up, walked up a mountain to
the cities castle (plenty of moaning - mainly from me) and jumped a train to Zagreb. We
were greeted by a moody Yank and bunch of the messiest Jocks we've met
to date and found a bar for the footie. A few Pans later we were on
the tram heading out of town to have a session with the locals and a
good sing song. Jonny was on lead vocals to Dirty Old Town, Stevie B
let the side down by failing to capture on film and took a penalty shot (his only one to date might I add!) but the scene was captured by a local documentary maker - rumor is its premiering in Ilfords Cineplex next year!  All in all a legendary night for Jimbotours as we were accepted among the locals in record time...

Today has been a struggle... we have moved on to a new hostel, sweated out alot of Jaga, the bookies are making a killing off poor tips from Jimbo and we are now gearing up to see England no doubt disappoint... 

Big drink is on the cards by the lake with an early flight to Split looming tomorrow!

Pictures to come....

Shauny G
