THE GREATEST DRINKING GAME IN THE WORLD...

BERLIN OR BUST  ·  0 photographs

We`ve invented the greatest drinking game in the world in Vienna. Here it goes:

You need:

1 x Pannini EURO 2008 Sticker Album

10 x Packets of Euro 2008 Stickers

20 x Mini bottles of Jagermyster

How to play:

Everyone sit round a table.

First person opens a packet of stickers. You then take a sticker and check if its needed. If the sticker is needed atfer sticking it in you down the shot. If the sticker is a swapsey you dont drink. Simple!

Also if 3 stickers in a row go in the direction the book goes round reverses.

If you get a sticker that completes a page in the book or a team evryone round the table does a shot and not you.

If you get the magical but illusive Number 1 sticker everyone, including you, do a shot in celebration.

This is the greatest game in the world - and also we get our sticker album filled in!! Only 117 to go!!!
