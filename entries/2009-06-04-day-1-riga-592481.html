DAY 1 - Riga

WHAT A LOADA BALTICS  ·  25 photographs

Wow - we have travelled a lot this week and have now reached our final destination, Riga. We got the bus up from Vilnius which took about 4 hours - was sad leaving Vilnius as its a great little town but weather was shit.

Got to Riga round 5. Checked into a wicked hostel called The Naughty Squirrel and we were ready to hit the town. Had a surprise for the lads as Jimbotours had its first ever mystery quest who was Danny who flew in today.

At night to celebrate g budd birthday we went on the town to this b ar the guy at the hostel recommended so we avoided the stag do's. at the pub 5pints of beer and 2 shot's was 3pounds!!! So we stayed there till 3am.
