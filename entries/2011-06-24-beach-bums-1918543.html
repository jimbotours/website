Beach Bums

JT - IT GROWS BUT DOES NOT AGE  ·  19 photographs

So hear we are now in Varna a resort on the black sea.

The weather has been blinding so after we checked into the hostel it was straight to the beach to get the aches and pains out of our legs after the exsertions in Sofia.

After a big blow down the beach we headed for Golden sands which is a resort up the coast. To describe it is basically blackpool in the sun for german and scandinavian people. Horrible!!

We had a few games or shauns new drinking game / brilliant and we were joined by our surprise guest / G Budd *more from him later.

Too many drinks were consumed and John and Danny decided to go swimming at 3 in the morning at a swimming pool in a bar / pictures and video evidence available / nuff said. Shaun and Jimbo went to the obligatory casino and left it behind before getting to bed at 5am.

very messy / very smelly room!!!
