BANG! Another BIG DRINK!

EAST MEETS EAST  ·  37 photographs
BOSH! After a very strange first night in Prague we were determined to make the most of night number 2! We decided the only way to see the real posh packer sites was to get on yet another bar crawl! We hit the drinking sites of Prague that we clearly missed last night, starting off in an American bar! After the first few beers going down a bit funny we realised that shots were definatley neeeded to get us through - a game of coins was the only way to go! With a shot of Sambuca going to the loser - 4 games later and the score was: Jimbo - 0 Stevie - 0 Shauny G - 2 Johnny - 2 Shaun + John boy were suddenly well up for it - BANG! Next bar was a bit "Brits on the piss" but we made the most of it with plenty more beer and a good few Sambucas! The quote of the tour so far came from a clearly drunk Johnny OD after spotting yet another Yankee hottie: Johnny 22:45 "In room 32 Ill either be puking or riding - or maybe both!" Went on to a big club where Shauny G was out of it and the 3 amigos were after anything in a skirt! Was messy to say the least! Ill let the pictures tell the rest of the story! I think the Sambuca got the best of us tonight but it wont stop us tommorrow on yet antoher pub crawl! Right Im cooked - time for bed at 4am! Shauny G!
