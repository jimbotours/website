If you know you know….

JT14-Wonders of the White City  ·  2 photographs

Which I didn’t 4 days ago as I was JD tour virgin. 

What a brilliant few days. 

As we board to go home a few reflections. 

New experiences are underrated, they’re good for the soul. Being out of your comfort zone is the only way to grow. Not speaking the local language relies a lot on hand movement and broken English.

It’s a true experience, it’s real. There are no bells and whistles, it’s organic. Its not for those that don’t want to be involved with the locals.

This is not for those that have to have creature comforts and that’s another beauty. 5 or 6 (think maybe 7) of us, some strangers, all in the same room. Interesting noises. 

Adds to the uniqueness.

It hopefully is the 1st of many.

Top jobs boys and thanks JImbo.
