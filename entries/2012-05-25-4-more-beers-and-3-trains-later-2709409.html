4 MORE BEERS, and 3 trains later.......

JT6- The Tour That Had No Name  ·  9 photographs

After a long lie in we set out on our second days adventures through Cologne.

After a consultation with a man in McDonalds who points us in the right direction for stuff to do.

We head off to do a spot of sightseeing and a general wonder, after doing the bridge with all the locks (which i still feel is a bit weird especially the ones who put kids pictures on) we head back to the Cathederal to do the epic climb.

Now minus Shaunny G (who was consulting with BetFred) we head up!!! it was nearly the death of me!!!!! warm days, very tight spiral stairs are not a good combination for me.

To make up for the Cathederal we then push on to relax at the beer garden in the park as recommended by the McDonalds man. What can i say, the park is awesome and the talent levels are exceptional

A short stay in the park completed it was off home to get sorted for the evening and to ready for the first play of the infamous sticker book game.

Needless to say, copious Jager was consumed and lots of craic was had, until I reached the state that i had to call it a night and head home.

What the others got up to i dont know and i'm not sure i want to.
