WE'VE LANDED!!

BERLIN OR BUST  ·  0 photographs

At 9:44am local time Slovakia held its breath and bar owners across Europe re-stocked their fridges as the East meets East team landed.

After a stress free journey to Bratislava we`ve had a big blow and are ready for a night on the tiles. It`s bloody hot out here and very cheap - sound`s like Stevie. Hostel is OK  but an old scottish lady on our floor is a bit wary of us as when we walked in she didn`t say hello - just "you`re not another bloody stag party are you?". Obviously we said yes so for the next few nights in Bratislava shaun will be hand-cuffed naked to various lamp-posts and statues around the city when drunk - photo`s to follow!!!

Out on the razz now so we`ll update with photo`s later.
