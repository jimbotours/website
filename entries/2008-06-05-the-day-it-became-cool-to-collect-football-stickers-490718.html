The day it became cool to collect football stickers

BERLIN OR BUST  ·  16 photographs

Two day´s down in Vienna, Stevie B´s romance seems to have turned into a one night stand - he´s getting over it with Jager

Random night last night, we all meet in an Aussie bar called Crossfield, Big Burgers all round which meant big smiles

The decision had been made to keep it to an early one, after a brief gasp from Shunie G, everyone bought in

Time for a shot and round of beers

Johnny loses first two, happy face gone, it was back after we´d finshed the 40th one

The panini game was invented, see seperate entry, a lot of confused faces from locals to start but they were just jealous of the fun we were having

By the end of the weekend, the book will be full and all sorts of nationalities will be on the vibe.

We´ve now left Elizabeth´s place and are officially posh packing - room even has tv and free towels

Big feed tonight, the O´Donnell´s are cooking!
