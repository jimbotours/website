Galway Geezers

JT15 - Craic Agus Ceol  ·  36 photographs

And like that….

It was done.

There are party towns and then there is Galway. 

2 nights in this westerly Irish city have been completed and I think I can speak for all of us when I say the experience won’t be forgotten quickly. 

The people are extremely welcoming and friendly and everyone seems to be having the time of their lives.

There are pubs a plenty and they take the time and care to ensure that the pint is of the quality that you hear about. 

The vibe was one of fun, laughs and forgetting about life for a while. 

We sampled the sea (cold), the food, (mixed) and the drink, great.

We’re now heading south to Westport via a walk up crow patrick.

I for one are looking forward to seeing what other adventures there are out there.
