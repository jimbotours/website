2007-06-15

Callum Best aka Stevie B

EAST MEETS EAST  ·  0 photographs
Background - Stevie B always makes one purchase before leaving for holiday - usually a shirt (a pulling one!) - He got the name in Barcelona Nov `05 for his antics! As you`ll see from the attached pictures - day 2 - the shirt comes out and with it Stevie B becomes Callum B Random women all over him, or CB all over them (his wandering hands will get him in trouble!) Anyway, the yanks have taken a shine to him, the aussies treat him as one of their own, the germans are scared of him and the Croats think it`s standard practice for a guy to put his arm round you and touch your breast while pretending not to realise. Well the shirt has been framed now and maybe auctioned at Christy`s on our return. Enjoy the photos
