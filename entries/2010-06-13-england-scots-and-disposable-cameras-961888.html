2010-06-13

England, Scots and disposable cameras

THE ROAD TO ROME  ·  9 photographs

So the trip continued on with another night in Zagreb.

After lying in our sweat box of a 1st hotel we made the decision to push on and get out of bed!

After a shower all round and spying the disposable camera of the extremely messy Scottish guys there was a team decision to leave them a souvenir, of sorts. So we left them a picture of Jimbo at his finest, and nakedest!!!!

After a long sit-down we made our way to our second hostel in Zagreb, after dodging a few very posh packing Brits we ducked out to watch the Eng-er-land match!!

With a bite of grub from the Micro brewery we headed up the up to a bar where we could watch the footie, as scouted out by Shaunie G and Johnny with a good view of the tele-box!

So we watched the footie and the cute waitress" ass as she went up the steps with a few warm beers and a great laugh at Rob Green! DODGY KEEPER!!!!!!  Even the locals laughed at him and were chewering on USA!

We then went about our normal Jimbo Tours a few more beers were had, thankfully chilled to perfection this time!  A few beers later and we (OK well, me) were chilling by the fountain and Almost going head first into the drink!

and then back to our hostel, which despite having air-con fitted to all communal areas they had made the decision to NOT fit it in the rooms, which provided us with sweat box number 2!!! not pretty in the morning, and probably very annoying for the people who got woke up by my alarm while I was out at the toilet!

So another early start then off to Split and hopefully not another sweat box
