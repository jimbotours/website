JIMBO TOURS 2009

BERLIN OR BUST  ·  1 photographs

WE`RE BACK - AND THIS TIME IS PERSONAL!!

Its that time of year again  - time to organise the tour of Europe 2009.

There are a few things we need to organise (such as where we`re going). As always Jimbotours is a democracry so the schedule is set out by the members of Jimbotours. To assist in this can all people emailed post on the message board of this site the answers to the following questions by midnight Friday 13th March 2009 (mine is there already):

1. Are you up for it?

Johhny - Standard

2. one week or two?

Johnny - one

3. It has already been decided that on the first night we will be staying in the Generator Hostel in Kings Cross. This will be a Friday. what day do we start this years` tour?

Johnny - Happy with a Friday

4. Last year we managed 4 countries - how many do we commit to this year?

Johnny - I reckon we could make 3 cities as well as London

5. Name 2 cities we should visit?

Johnny - 2 ideas. 1 - Keeping it East(ish) - Riga, Talin, Helsinki or 2. - Going West - France, Lux, Amsterdam

6. give a brief plan of your 2009 tour?

Johnny - As we`ve historically mixed up the modes of transport, my Eastish option would involve flying to Riga, train to Talin, Ferry to Helsinki and fly home.  The second option would include posh packing Eurostar to France, Train through to Luxembourg (or maybe Dortmund), train to Amsterdam and ferry home.

Johnny - towel

10. should there be a mystery city? -

Look forward to your responses - then we can book flights and the generator.
