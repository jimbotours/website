QUESTIONNAIRE 2010

In traditional Jimbotours fashion 2010's questionnaire to sum up the best & the wierdest moments of the tour....

Johnny - I've got to go for Ljubljana - I think we left a bit behind there

G-Budd - Tough, but ROME (for the architecture and of course the walking Jimbotours)

Jimbo - The dark beer in brewery in zagreb

Shauny - Pan in first bar in Zagreb (G - I thought yours would have been "my fantasy...?"

Shauny - Don't know if this technically counts...  but Jimboland is my favourite moment!

Stevie B - Little Zagreb Boozer

G-Budd - Anywhere on the river bank in Rome

Jimbo - Random night in middle of no-where with band. unfortunately steve cant use a camera so it goes unrecorded

Johnny - Zagreb nightclub - the pictures said it all

G-Budd - The last one. Every thing was perfect. Stunning scenery, good drinks and beautiful (Italian, Brazilian) women. Europe can be truly amazing.

Johnny -

20. BIG QUESTION – JIMBOTOUR’s 07, 08, 09 or 10???

Jimbo - Had a long good think. For sheer diversity and good weather its 2010 by a whisker.

Shauny - Its tough... 08 was a masterclass in touring plus had the football....  10 had the weather, the sites & the footie...  For me the banter was great this year... 2010 has it (but only just!)

Stevie B - Has to be 2010

Johnny -

G-Budd - Its real close. 2008 was a footballing, sticker experience - never to be matched. 2009 was Euro disco heaven and 2010 was Jimbotours finally coming-of-age. So i'm gonna go for 2010, by a pube!

21. EXTRA QUESTION - MAN OR BODY PART OF THE TOUR

G-Budd - There was a clear winner. James feet. Performed excellent under pressure. Sometimes we forgot they were there!

Shauny - Stevie B through it away in injury time so for me Jimbos feet take it for an excellent performance - I hear that Man City are interested in signing them next season!

Stevie B - Shaun's thumbs (and fingers)! for all the work they put in ensuring he always had his bets on!

Johnny -

James - my feet were amazing. Steve had it wrapped up halfway through but fell 2 out. Gotta be Shaun for getting up those last flight of stairs in Rome to find out the church at the top was closed.
