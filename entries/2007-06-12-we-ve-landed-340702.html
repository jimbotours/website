We've landed.......

EAST MEETS EAST  ·  0 photographs
.........and like any great travellers the first we did was get some fast food and go to bed for a sleep!! We`re only travelling for 10 days but we decided to set this site up for 2 reasons: 1, Its cheaper to post entries than ring home 2, We`re likely to remember very little when we get home as we finish in Krakow and our little brains will be blanked out sampling the local vodka so we can use this site a a mind filler-in!! The travelling party is split down the middle into 2 groups at the moment on one side we have the "posh-packers" (Shaun and John) with their Gucci sandles and Designer bags and me and steve with 1 pair of pants between us. Our hostel in Germany is nice although they don`t provide towels - unfortuantely me the experienced traveller told evryone they would!! So there will be a lot of standing close together in the morning after showers to dry au naturale. We`re off on a pub crawl round Munich tonight so this is likely t be the last post as i`m sure i won`t remember setting this up in the morning........ .....so if i don`t im sure the rest of our trip was very amazing. J
