Let the Games begin

BERLIN OR BUST  ·  18 photographs

east

So this is it - i`ve been here now 5 days with the lads, everything`s hurting, alot of food has been consumed (we`ve all put on 3 stone, except Shaun who refuses to eat anything but rice cakes) and various toilets have taken an absolute battering.

oh and the y key`s on a different place on this keyboard!

Yesterday was an absolute treat, no in fact it was more than that, it was a meaty treat.

James pulled out another walking tour, it was worth 14 Euros in anyone`s book, not like the first one though cos i didn`t swap any fussball stcikers.

A couple of churches, a few monks, not enough pretzels for my liking and more beer than a normal human can drink.

The fan zone was amazing as well - 9 out of 10

Today`s its gonna be better cos its gonna be full of Croatians and Austrians.

Gotta go, help me
