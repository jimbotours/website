2007-06-15

Change of Country

EAST MEETS EAST  ·  0 photographs
After an eventfull night train from Munich we arrived in Prague at 9 oclock this morning. It is well hot out here at the moment and the little carriage we had last was absolutly boiling. 2 Japanesse girls had the other 2 bunks in our 6 bed dorms and they got on about an hour into the journey. The smells and noises coming from the bunks below them must have been awful. Dont worry we didnt let down the good image of Engish abroad as we prentented we were Americans!! So far Prague is very Hot. The Hostel is basic and the they love a big serving of meat. Also worth mentioing that beer is about 15 to 30p!!!! Tonight we out on the town. No ones fallen out yet but their were some grumpy people this morning getting ff the train after sweating out pints of fliud through the night (not me) Anyway we`l upload some cool pics of stevie B on the pull on our last nght in Munich when we can find a usp port. j
